FAQs

Who can refer?

Anyone! Friends, alumni, coworkers—if your referral gets hired, you get paid.

When do I get paid?

Usually after the new hire completes 30–90 days. Each job will list payout terms clearly.

What does it cost to post a job?

Posting is free. You only pay the bounty when you hire someone.
